# GHSS Mahadevpur – Official School Website

This repository contains the source code for the official website of  
**Government Higher Secondary School Mahadevpur (GHSS)**,  
a CBSE affiliated, English medium, co-educational school under the  
**Directorate of School Education, Government of Arunachal Pradesh**.

---

## 🌐 About the School

**GHSS Mahadevpur** was established in **1956** and has been providing quality education with a focus on national integration and all-round development of students.  
The school offers **Science and Humanities streams** for **Classes IX to XII**.

**Motto:**  
> *"Come to Learn, Go to Serve"*

---

## 🚀 Features of the Website

- Responsive modern UI
- Animated homepage
- Admissions page
- Academic streams (Science & Humanities)
- Co-curricular activities (NCC, NSS, Scouts & Guides)
- News & Announcements
- Photo Gallery
- Notes & Syllabus (Class 9–12)
- Student & Teacher Login system
- Smooth scrolling and professional CBSE-style design

---
