An Ongoing Journey of Excellence

GHSS Mahadevpur began its journey as a Primary School in 1956 and has grown significantly over the decades under the Directorate of School Education, Government of Arunachal Pradesh.

The school was upgraded to Middle School in 1967, then to Secondary School in 1978, and finally to Higher Secondary School in 1988.

Today, it is a CBSE affiliated English medium, Co-educational School offering Classes IX to XII with Science and Humanities streams.
